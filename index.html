Generated by pnpm shots:publish. Screenshots are captured at 2x, so a 1512px capture is 3024 image pixels wide. Every file below is live at its listed URL and can be pasted straight into an Amplience content item. Each section also has a thumbnail-140.png — its desktop capture in a 1.41:1 frame — and a card.html that sizes it to fill its card slot. card.html is the URL to paste into the Amplience content-type card, never the PNG: a Custom card pointed at an image renders it unsized and clipped.
